# Sound effects

The sources below are CC0. Credits are included voluntarily. Some clips have been converted or shortened.

## Used by the game

- Wood and construction: rubberduck, [100 CC0 Metal and Wood SFX](https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-metal-and-wood-sfx).
- Zombie: saturn91, [Zomby sfx pack](https://opengameart.org/content/zomby-sfx-pack).
- Watchtower projectile and projectile hit: Vehicle, [Fantasy Sound Effects (Tinysized SFX)](https://opengameart.org/content/fantasy-sound-effects-tinysized-sfx); projectile hit shortened with fade.
- Lantern Flare: Julien Matthey, [Fireball](https://opengameart.org/content/fireball-1).
- Combat impact: StarNinjas, [10 Impact/Shield Blocks](https://opengameart.org/content/10-impactshield-blocks).
- Stone: qubodup, [Gathering Stone Resources](https://freesound.org/people/qubodup/sounds/184383/). Shortened from Freesound's HQ MP3 preview, not the original FLAC.
- Food: Magnesus, [Rusle of leaves](https://freesound.org/people/Magnesus/sounds/729439/). Freesound HQ MP3 preview, not the original FLAC.
- Victory: cynicmusic, [Victory Fanfare Short](https://opengameart.org/content/victory-fanfare-short).
- Defeat: Kistol, [Game Over IV](https://opengameart.org/content/game-over-iv).
